Utf8StreamDecoder

class Utf8StreamDecoder(source)

Turns a stream of byte chunks into text without breaking multi-byte characters.

A model token is a piece of UTF-8, not a character: an emoji or an accented letter can arrive split across two tokens. Decoding every token on its own would print "�". This keeps the unfinished tail of a character until the rest of its bytes arrive.
